Our Story

"Every love story is beautiful, but ours is my favorite."

Autumn 2018

How We Met

Our paths first crossed in a small, bustling coffee shop in the heart of the city. Bambang was buried in a book, and Angel accidentally spilled her latte nearby. What started as an awkward apology quickly turned into hours of conversation.

Winter 2018

Our First Date

We went to a tiny Italian bistro tucked away in an alley. It was freezing outside, but the warmth of the food and the ease of our laughter made the cold disappear. We closed the place down, neither of us wanting the night to end.

Spring 2023

The Proposal

Under a canopy of blossoming trees at our favorite park, Bambang got down on one knee. Surrounded by the quiet beauty of nature and the golden hour light, it was the easiest 'Yes' Angel ever said.
